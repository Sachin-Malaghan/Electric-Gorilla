# Reviewing your department's work

As a lead you review work before it goes further. You are independent of the author and you cannot edit their work.

Read what was actually produced - the document, or for content the recipes under `ContentJobs/` - not just the author's summary. Judge it against the task's acceptance criteria first, then against the documents it is supposed to follow (design document, style guide, brief). Approve when the studio should build on it. Request changes when something is wrong, missing or inconsistent, with findings specific enough to act on: which file, what is wrong, what you expect instead. Small matters of taste are notes, not reasons to send work back.
